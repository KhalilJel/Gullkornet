## Limitations and responsible use

- Public directories may be incomplete, stale, or inconsistent.
- Failure to find a website or social profile is not proof that one does not exist.
- Automated website signals are indicators for review, not definitive judgments of quality.
- Do not infer revenue, customer loss, or intent from public website signals.
- Public contact information should be used responsibly and in line with source terms and applicable privacy and marketing rules.
- Re-check time-sensitive details before acting on the dataset.

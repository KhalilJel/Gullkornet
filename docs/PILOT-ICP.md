# Cidea Pilot ICP

## Target account

Small Norwegian accounting firms and bookkeeping practices operating in Oslo or Akershus.

## Why this segment is being tested

Accounting firms sell trust-based, high-value professional services. Their websites can help potential clients understand service lines, credibility, target customers, and how to request a conversation. That creates specific, inspectable opportunities for a website redesign.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not proof that accounting firms are the best segment. The pilot should compare the quality of actual leads and response to outreach.

## Positive signals

- Independent or small-to-medium accounting/bookkeeping practice
- Publicly identifiable business website or evidence that the business has no website
- One specific website issue that can be demonstrated from a public source
- Clear public business contact route
- Located in Oslo or Akershus
- Services and company activity are still current

## Website opportunity categories

- NO_WEBSITE: no official company website could be found after a documented check
- OUTDATED_DESIGN: concrete usability or presentation issues are visible
- UNCLEAR_SERVICES: visitors cannot easily understand the firm's services or intended clients
- WEAK_CONTACT_PATH: contact or enquiry route is hard to find or unnecessarily difficult
- MOBILE_USABILITY: a reproducible mobile layout or usability issue is observed
- PERFORMANCE: a repeatable performance problem is measured using a recorded source
- NONE: no clear opportunity found; do not qualify

## Evidence requirements

Every claimed opportunity must have:
- A source URL
- A specific, neutral observation
- The date checked where available
- No unsupported assumptions about revenue, customer satisfaction, or business performance

Use wording such as "The service page does not show a visible enquiry button" rather than "The website is losing customers."

## Exclusion rules

- Outside the pilot geography
- Not an accounting/bookkeeping business
- No documented website opportunity
- No reliable source evidence
- Already marked DO_NOT_CONTACT
- Duplicate domain or contact email
- Business appears closed or inactive

## Pilot evaluation

Research 50 businesses. Manually review every lead marked qualified. Track:
- Percentage with accurate company details
- Percentage with a real, demonstrable website opportunity
- Percentage with a valid public business contact route
- Outreach replies and positive replies after approved contact
- Meetings booked and paid work won

Do not expand the target segment based only on lead volume. Expand when the evidence and business outcomes justify it.

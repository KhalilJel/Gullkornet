# Masterplan 2026 — Cidea + SmartSvar

## Operating model

Build two customer-facing businesses with one shared internal growth engine.

### Cidea — Digital Growth

Offers:
- Websites
- Branding / visual identity
- SEO and digital marketing
- Social media setup and content
- Lead generation

Primary short-term role: generate revenue through concrete, easy-to-understand digital improvements.

### SmartSvar — AI Solutions

Offers:
- AI Consulting
- AI Audit / AI efficiency analysis
- AI Agent Development
- AI automation and ongoing optimisation

Primary role: identify and implement profitable AI use cases for businesses.

### Gullkornet — Internal sales engine

Gullkornet is an internal system for discovering, researching, deduplicating and prioritising potential customers for Cidea and, later, SmartSvar.

It is not a customer-facing product at this stage.

### Managed AI Cybersecurity — future

Keep as a future service line. Do not market it as a managed security service until competence, scope, tooling, responsibility and delivery quality are ready. Basic security and responsible AI practices apply to every AI project from day one.

## First five steps

### Step 1 — Freeze the commercial architecture

Define exactly what Cidea and SmartSvar sell first.

Cidea:
- Digital Website Upgrade
- New Website
- Ongoing Digital Growth

SmartSvar:
- AI Opportunity Audit
- AI Agent Pilot
- AI Automation / Ongoing AI Optimisation

Each offer must have a clear customer problem, deliverable, timeline and test price.

Decision rule: no new service is added to the front page until a customer problem justifies it.

### Step 2 — Make both sales messages simple

Cidea message:
"Vi hjelper bedrifter å bli mer synlige og få flere henvendelser digitalt."

SmartSvar message:
"Vi hjelper bedrifter å finne og implementere AI som faktisk sparer tid eller skaper verdi."

The websites should lead with outcomes, proof and a clear call to action rather than a long technology catalogue.

### Step 3 — Build the first sales pipeline

Use Gullkornet to create a controlled pipeline of high-fit prospects.

Initial workflow:
1. Discover 20–30 businesses in one niche/geography.
2. Verify company and website.
3. Capture evidence-backed digital opportunities.
4. Prioritise the best prospects.
5. Prepare a short, personalised reason to start a conversation.

Outbound sending remains manual/controlled during the pilot. Do not change DNS/MX or activate uncontrolled bulk sending.

### Step 4 — Sell two paid pilots

Cidea:
Sell a concrete digital improvement project with a short delivery cycle.

SmartSvar:
Sell one paid AI Opportunity Audit with a concrete business process as the focus.

The purpose is not to prove the complete business model. The purpose is to learn what customers will pay for, what delivery actually costs, and what naturally leads to follow-up work.

### Step 5 — Turn what sells into systems

After the first customer work:
- document the delivery;
- measure hours, costs, price and margin;
- identify repeated tasks;
- automate only validated tasks;
- turn successful projects into recurring offers;
- use case studies and evidence in future sales.

## 30-day success criteria

The first 30 days are successful when:
- there are active, qualified prospects for both businesses;
- at least one Cidea offer has been sold or has a concrete proposal in market;
- at least one SmartSvar pilot has been actively sold;
- we know which offer gets the strongest response;
- no major production automation is built before customer demand is proven.

## Guardrails

- No DNS/MX changes.
- No uncontrolled live email automation.
- No SmartSvar production changes as part of the sales-engine pilot.
- No unsupported customer claims.
- No treating automated website flags as proof of commercial opportunity.

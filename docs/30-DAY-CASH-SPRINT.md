# Cidea 30-Day Cash Sprint

**Objective:** Collect at least NOK 50,000 within 30 days from website projects.
**Owner:** Khalil handles every sales conversation.
**Operating rule:** Sales conversations and delivery take priority over new Gullkornet features.
**Safety:** This is a sales playbook only. It does not send email, change DNS/MX, or interact with SmartSvar production.

## Cash target and offer

Treat NOK 50,000 as **cash received**, not pipeline value or unsigned proposals.

Test one clear, scoped offer:
- **Website Launch:** from NOK 19,900, subject to a written scope.
- One-page or small-business website, mobile-responsive layout, clear services, contact path, basic on-page SEO setup, and launch support.
- **Payment:** 60% to reserve the project and start; 40% before launch/hand-off.
- Five projects at NOK 19,900 would represent NOK 99,500 in contracted revenue; five 60% deposits would represent NOK 59,700 collected if all deposits are paid. This is a target scenario, not a forecast.
- Do not promise a fixed price before checking scope. Exclude hosting, paid tools, copywriting beyond the agreed scope, advanced integrations, and ongoing maintenance unless explicitly included.

For a prospect who needs less, offer a separately scoped landing page or website improvement sprint. Do not discount automatically; reduce scope instead.

## Daily operating rhythm

Every weekday:
1. **09:00–10:00:** Follow up all warm replies and previous conversations.
2. **10:00–12:00:** Research 10–15 businesses and identify one verifiable website issue for each.
3. **12:30–14:00:** Send individually reviewed outreach, only where permitted; no bulk or unreviewed sending.
4. **14:00–15:30:** Call suitable businesses, respond to replies, and book short discovery conversations.
5. **15:30–16:30:** Send tailored proposals and invoices/deposit requests to qualified buyers.
6. **16:30–17:00:** Update the pipeline and define tomorrow's highest-value follow-ups.

Track daily: researched, contacted, replies, positive replies, conversations, proposals, deposits paid, cash collected, and delivery commitments.

## 30-day checkpoints

### Days 1–2: Convert existing interest
- Review and personally follow up the three replies from the first 45 manually sent emails.
- Identify what each person actually needs before proposing a website.
- Send a specific next step and a concise scope/pricing range only when relevant.
- Review the 15 contact-path findings in the latest Gullkornet review queue first, then the remaining evidence-backed findings.
- Build a short list of the 20 most promising prospects, manually verifying each website and contact route.

### Days 3–7: Close the first project
- Contact 10–15 researched businesses per working day with individual observations.
- Use direct calls as well as email when appropriate.
- Offer a short, practical explanation of the specific website improvement, not a generic sales pitch.
- Send proposals within 24 hours of a qualified conversation.
- Goal: first signed scope and paid deposit by day 7. This is an execution target, not a guarantee.

### Days 8–14: Repeat what gets responses
- Review which segment, evidence, and message generated genuine interest.
- Keep the strongest segment; stop spending time on weak-fit leads.
- Ask satisfied customers and existing business contacts for relevant introductions.
- Keep project delivery small and predictable so sales activity continues.

### Days 15–21: Fill delivery slots
- Follow up all open proposals with a clear, polite question.
- Prioritize buyers who have a defined need, decision-maker access, and ability to start.
- Request deposits only after scope, timing, and deliverables are agreed in writing.

### Days 22–30: Collect and deliver
- Complete agreed milestones promptly.
- Invoice according to the agreed payment schedule and follow up unpaid invoices professionally.
- Continue prospecting until collected cash reaches NOK 50,000; do not treat verbal interest or unpaid invoices as cash.

## Qualification checklist

Before sending a proposal, confirm:
- The business has a real, observed website issue or a stated website need.
- The person is involved in the decision.
- The intended outcome and scope are clear.
- The customer understands price, timeline, responsibilities, and payment milestones.
- The delivery can realistically be completed without jeopardizing existing commitments.

## Non-negotiables
- No unsupported claims about lost revenue, conversion lifts, or guaranteed results.
- No mass sending without review. Verify each observation and recipient.
- Respect opt-outs and do-not-contact requests; comply with applicable marketing and privacy rules.
- No live email sending from Gullkornet until separately reviewed and explicitly enabled.
- No DNS/MX changes and no SmartSvar production changes.
- Keep the pipeline honest: interest is not a sale; a proposal is not revenue; only cleared payments count toward the cash target.

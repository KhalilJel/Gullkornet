# Cidea — Initial Sales Offers

Cidea is the Digital Growth business: websites, branding, SEO/digital marketing, social media and lead generation.

The goal is to sell a small number of clear outcomes rather than a long service catalogue.

## Offer A — Digital Improvement

For businesses that already have a website but a clear, evidence-backed opportunity to improve.

Deliver:
- focused website / conversion improvements;
- clearer service presentation;
- stronger contact or enquiry path;
- selected SEO or content improvements where relevant.

Pilot price: 5,000–10,000 NOK depending on scope.

Use when the prospect has a specific, visible problem that can be fixed quickly.

## Offer B — New Website

For businesses that need a new digital presence or a substantial redesign.

Deliver:
- positioning and page structure;
- visual direction;
- responsive website;
- conversion-focused contact journey;
- launch support.

Pilot price: 15,000–30,000 NOK depending on scope.

## Offer C — Digital Growth

For businesses that want ongoing help after the initial project.

Potential scope:
- SEO;
- content;
- social media setup and content;
- landing pages;
- lead generation;
- monthly improvement work.

Pilot price: 3,000–8,000 NOK/month depending on scope.

## Sales rule

Start with the smallest credible offer that solves the observed problem. Do not sell a full rebuild when a smaller intervention is enough.

Every outbound opportunity must have:
- a verified business identity;
- a source URL;
- a dated observation;
- a clear hypothesis about the opportunity.

No unsupported claim such as "you have no website" should be used when the evidence has not been verified.

## Primary message

"Vi hjelper bedrifter å bli mer synlige og få flere henvendelser digitalt."

The outreach should open a conversation, not attempt to close the whole sale in the first message.

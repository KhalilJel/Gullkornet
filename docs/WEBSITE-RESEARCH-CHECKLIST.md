# Website opportunity research checklist

Use this checklist for each NEW registry candidate. Do not mark a lead QUALIFIED until a concrete opportunity and source evidence have been recorded.

## Verify the business

- [ ] Business is active in the public business registry.
- [ ] Organization number and company name match the official record.
- [ ] Business operates in the Oslo/Akershus pilot area.
- [ ] Business activity matches accounting/bookkeeping services.
- [ ] Official website and public business contact route are identified where available.

## Review the website

Check the homepage and at least one relevant service/contact page when available.

- [ ] Website exists and appears to belong to the business.
- [ ] Main services are understandable to a prospective customer.
- [ ] Intended customer or service area is reasonably clear.
- [ ] Contact/enquiry route is visible and works.
- [ ] Mobile layout is usable at a narrow viewport.
- [ ] Design and content appear maintained and consistent.
- [ ] If making a performance claim, a measurement tool and result are recorded.
- [ ] If no website is found, record where and how the search was performed; do not treat a registry record alone as proof that no website exists.

## Record the opportunity

Choose one primary issue:
- `NO_WEBSITE`
- `OUTDATED_DESIGN`
- `UNCLEAR_SERVICES`
- `WEAK_CONTACT_PATH`
- `MOBILE_USABILITY`
- `PERFORMANCE`
- `NONE`

For every issue other than `NONE`, add:
- A source URL that supports the observation
- A concise, factual observation
- The date checked
- A practical improvement Cidea could offer

Good observation:
> The service page lists accounting services but does not provide a clear next step for requesting a consultation.

Avoid unsupported claims:
> The company is losing customers because of its website.

## Qualification decision

- If industry or location is wrong, do not qualify.
- If no specific website opportunity is found, use `NONE` and do not qualify.
- If the evidence is incomplete, keep the lead as `NEW` or `RESEARCHED`, not `QUALIFIED`.
- If a public contact email is not available, record another official business contact route separately for human review rather than guessing an email address.
- Check the existing CRM/store and do-not-contact status before any future outreach.

## Quality control

Manually review every qualified lead during the initial 50-business pilot. Prioritize evidence accuracy and the likelihood that the business has a genuine need over the number of records collected.

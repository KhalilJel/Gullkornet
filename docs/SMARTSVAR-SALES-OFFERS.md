# SmartSvar — Initial Sales Offers

SmartSvar is the AI Solutions business.

Its first public services are AI Consulting, AI Agent Development and AI Automation.

## Offer A — AI Opportunity Audit

Purpose: identify one or two practical AI opportunities inside a business and estimate potential value before development.

Deliver:
- process review;
- opportunity map;
- priority recommendation;
- indicative business case;
- implementation plan.

Pilot price: 7,500–12,500 NOK.

The analysis must distinguish observed facts, customer-provided information and assumptions.

## Offer B — AI Agent Pilot

Purpose: build one focused AI agent or workflow that solves a validated business problem.

Deliver:
- scoped workflow;
- agent design;
- integrations where needed;
- test and handover;
- measurement criteria.

Pilot price: 15,000–50,000+ NOK depending on complexity.

## Offer C — AI Automation / Ongoing Optimisation

Purpose: keep improving proven AI workflows after the first implementation.

Potential scope:
- monitoring;
- prompt / workflow optimisation;
- new automations;
- additional integrations;
- ongoing AI advisory.

Price: scope after the first pilot.

## Customer journey

AI Opportunity Audit -> AI Agent Pilot -> Ongoing AI Automation.

The audit is the preferred first purchase when the customer knows AI matters but has not identified the right implementation.

## Primary message

"Vi hjelper bedrifter med å finne og implementere AI som faktisk skaper verdi."

Do not lead with model names, technical stacks or exaggerated ROI claims.

## Future service

Managed AI Cybersecurity remains outside the initial sales catalogue. It can be developed later after competence, tooling, scope and responsibility are clearly defined.
